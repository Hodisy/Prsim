# Amina — family airport demo

## Known context

- Amina is 35–44 and is travelling with two children.
- Preferred color is cream.
- She needs hands-free carrying and one-handed access to passports, headphones and small essentials.

## Relevant use

For this trip, share only the family-travel, children, cream, quick-access, shared-organisation and under-seat context. Do not infer the children’s ages or the airline.
