# Claire — cream Eurostar business variation

- Claire is 35–44 and is travelling from Paris to London for a client workshop.
- She needs laptop organisation, durable construction and a professional silhouette.
- She explicitly prefers cream because black feels too severe.
- She likes measured material proof but also wants practical care information.

Use the rail-business context while preserving the explicit cream preference and slightly warmer tone. Never redirect her to black because of business-use merchandising affinities.
