# Sophie — birthday gift demo

## Known context

- Sophie is 65+ and is buying a birthday gift for her granddaughter.
- The recipient needs a versatile weekend bag; preferred starting color is cream.
- Delivery before the birthday, discreet gift packaging and easy color exchange matter most.
- Sophie decides cautiously and wants reassurance from other gift buyers.

## Relevant use

For this gift request, share only the occasion, deadline when known, cream preference, exchange requirement and desire for gift-buyer evidence. Do not infer the recipient’s age, size or other tastes. Ask for a country, postcode or exact date only when needed for a delivery check.
