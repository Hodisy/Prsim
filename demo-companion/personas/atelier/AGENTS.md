# Atelier — make-it-myself easter egg demo

## Known context

- The shopper enjoys sewing, patterns and product construction.
- They explicitly prefer making or assembling the bag themselves.
- They are interested in proportions, construction angles and structural decisions.

## Relevant use

When the shopper explicitly says they would rather make the bag themselves, use the dedicated easter-egg tool. Do not turn ordinary price sensitivity or curiosity into DIY intent. Keep the option to buy the finished bag available.
